Online authorization and electronic permit issuance platform with QR validation and multi-tier approval workflows.

View Mobile Inspector App View Web Gallery

Project Overview

Tasareeh is a digital permit management web application developed to handle electronic requests, administrative reviews, compliance audits, and instant verification of commercial, environmental, and municipal work authorizations.

Key Capabilities

  • Dynamic Workflow Engine: Multi-stage application review pipeline with status alerts and digital signature stamps.
  • Encrypted QR Validation: Every generated permit includes a cryptographically secure QR code for on-site field inspections.
  • PDF Document Engine: Automated PDF permit rendering with official stamps and tamper-detection hashes.
