GoCrispy Food Chain

Online food ordering, dynamic multi-branch menu management, and real-time delivery tracking platform developed at M3soft.

Visit Live GoCrispy View Gallery

Project Overview

GoCrispy is a fast-food franchise chain providing customers with seamless online ordering, customizable meal combos, branch pickup scheduling, and doorstep delivery.

Built at M3soft with Laravel, Vue.js, and custom REST API integrations, the system bridges front-of-house customer web orders with back-of-house kitchen dispatch screens.

Technical Highlights

  • Branch-Specific Menus: Automated stock toggling and pricing rules based on customer location and selected branch.
  • Real-Time Cart & Checkout: Fast checkout flow with coupon redemption, delivery address validation, and payment gateway.
  • Kitchen Ticket Generation: Instant order transmission to POS and kitchen printer networks.
