Project Overview

Transport Hub is an enterprise solution designed for freight forwarders, fleet dispatchers, and cargo operators. It centralizes real-time load dispatch, carrier tracking, driver notifications, and automated billing.

As Full Stack Developer (Laravel) at Exon Technologies, I collaborated closely with the digital product and client teams to engineer the core application modules and automate internal business processes.

Engineering Highlights

  • Process Digitalization: Replaced manual spreadsheets and dispatch sheets with automated web workflows and interactive status boards.
  • UX & Performance Enhancements: Optimized page load speeds and interface responsiveness in close collaboration with the marketing and product teams.
  • Customer Support & Reliability: Diagnosed and resolved mission-critical production tickets, maintaining 99.9% uptime.
